Public mailing lists related to AI agents communities (with publicly readable archives or open subscription where noted):
IETF lists (public archives, open to discussion of AI agent protocols/discovery)

    agent2agent@ietf.org — Standardization of AI agent communications (discovery, conversation between agents, protocols such as MCP/A2A, possible BoF/WG).
    Subscribe/info: https://mailman3.ietf.org/mailman3/lists/agent2agent@ietf.org/
    Archives: https://mailarchive.ietf.org/arch/browse/agent2agent/
    Owner: agent2agent-owner@ietf.org⁠Mailman3.ietf
    dawn@ietf.org — Discovery of Agents, Workloads, and Named entities (DAWN; includes AI agents, tasks, endpoints using IETF technologies).
    Subscribe/info: https://mailman3.ietf.org/mailman3/lists/dawn.ietf.org/
    Archives: https://mailarchive.ietf.org/arch/browse/dawn/
    Owner: dawn-owner@ietf.org⁠Mailman3.ietf

Agent Community (agentcommunity.org) workstream lists

These are open mailing lists with public archives (threads visible without joining). They focus on standards, identity, security, trust, and governance for the agentic web.

Portal with links to each: https://agentcommunity.org/lists

Current workstreams (as of mid-2026):

    Evals (agent evaluation / best practices)
    Identity (agent identity)
    Security (agent security / best practices)
    Trust (verified agents, reputation, etc.)
    Governance (community governance; noted as private in some views)

Contact for collaboration / joining groups: collab@agentcommunity.org or hello@agentcommunity.org.

General community site: https://agentcommunity.org⁠Agentcommunity

Notes:

    Most active public technical discussion for AI agents happens on the two IETF lists above and the Agent Community workstream lists.
    Many other “AI agents communities” use Discord, Reddit (e.g. r/AI_Agents), Skool, or newsletters rather than traditional public mailing lists.
    Agent-specific email inboxes (e.g. AgentMail, agentsmail.org, etc.) are infrastructure for individual agents, not community mailing lists.

Subscribe via the linked info pages; public archives let you read without joining where available. Lists and membership can change.
